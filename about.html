ABA Behavior Counter

Configurable behavior tracking with daily data retained locally until you clear it.

What it does

The counter is designed to stay simple enough for fast use while still giving each user control over their own behavior list.

Custom behavior list

Add, edit, delete, import, export, or share the behaviors you want available on your device.

Frequency by time of day

Record Morning, Midday, and Afternoon frequency and view the daily total automatically.

Quick +1 counter

Select a behavior and time period, then use one large button for fast event counting.

Daily data stays available

Counts, context, date, setting, and notes remain available after closing the app until you choose Clear Daily Data.

Transfer lists

Share behavior names directly or import lists from pasted text, TXT, CSV, or JSON files.

No account required

Open the web app from Safari or Chrome and configure it directly on the device you are using.

Privacy by design: the configured behavior names and the current daily session data are stored in the browser’s local site storage so the information remains available after the app is closed and reopened. Daily data stays there until the user selects Clear Daily Data (or the browser/site storage is cleared). The app does not send session data to a server and does not request client-identifying information.

Add it to your Home Screen

Once added, ABA Behavior Counter opens from its own icon and feels much more like a standalone app.

  1. iPhone: open ABA Behavior Counter in Safari, tap Share, then choose Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: open it in Chrome, open the browser menu, then choose Add to Home screen or the available install option.
  3. Open the new ABA Counter icon from your Home Screen.
