Quick Start & How to Use
  1. Tap + Add Behavior and enter the behaviors you need to track.
  2. Tap a Morning, Midday, or Afternoon box for the behavior you want to count.
  3. Use +1 Counter each time the behavior occurs.
  4. Use Clear Daily Data at the end of the session. Your behavior list stays saved on this device.
